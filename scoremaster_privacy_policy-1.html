Privacy Policy

ScoreMaster Pro — Digital Scoreboard

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ScoreMaster Pro (“the App”), developed by ScoreMaster Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”), handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not ask you to create an account. Based on the current app functionality, we do not intentionally collect or request personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Information you enter into the scoreboard, such as team names, scores, timer settings, and round or set counts, is used to operate the App. If the App stores any such information, it is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server for collecting this scoreboard information.

3. Internet and Third-Party Services

The core scoreboard features are designed to work offline. This policy does not cover third-party websites or services that you may access separately. If a future version adds analytics, advertising, cloud backup, or other third-party services, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We do not intentionally transmit scoreboard entries to us or to third parties.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is a general-purpose scoreboard and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to us in connection with the App, please contact us so we can review the concern.

6. Data Security and Retention

Scoreboard information handled locally by the App remains subject to your device’s security, backup, and storage settings. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data may remove locally stored information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s features or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will be published at this same page with an updated date.

8. Contact

For questions or privacy-related requests about ScoreMaster Pro, contact the developer through the developer contact information shown on the App’s distribution page.
